Schedule

Every dive, every instructor, on one screen.

See the day by instructor, assign a whole group in two taps, and know each diver's language, level and gear sizes before they walk in.

QR check-in

Customers fill in their own paperwork.

Put a QR code on the counter. Divers enter their details, sizes, emergency contact and consent on their own phone. They appear in your customer list within a minute, ready to book.

  • No paper forms, no retyping
  • Returning divers are matched, never duplicated
  • Desired course and planned date captured
  • Your logo and colours on the form
Payments

Know who has paid, down to the rupiah.

Deposits, part payments, discounts at the till and refunds are tracked per booking. Marine park and eco fees are kept apart as cash, so your revenue stays clean.

  • Red when unpaid, green when collected
  • Paid bookings can't be deleted by mistake
  • Marine fees tracked separately, with paid confirmation
  • Add a fun dive on the spot for a returning diver
Team and payroll

Commissions that calculate themselves.

Set a percentage for courses and a rate per fun dive. Instructors declare extra activities, you approve them, and the monthly statement is ready to export. The pay pages sit behind their own password.

  • Course percentage and fun-dive rate, both editable
  • Declarations approved by a manager
  • One statement per instructor, in Excel

Questions

What dive centers ask us.

Is my customers' data safe?

Each dive center's data is kept separate and private. Only people you invite can sign in, and each person gets a role that limits what they can see. Data is encrypted in transit and stored on managed cloud infrastructure with automatic backups.

Does it work with PADI, or only SSI?

It works with any agency. Certification levels are a list you control, and each customer has a field for their certification number.

Do I need internet at the dive center?

Yes. Scubook runs in a web browser, so it needs a connection, but it is light enough for a normal mobile data plan.

Can I bring my existing customers and prices?

Yes. Customers and price lists can be imported from CSV or Excel files, and we help you do it during setup.

Can customers pay online?

Not yet. Scubook records the payments you take at the desk, by cash, card or transfer. Online booking and payment are on our roadmap.

How do instructors' commissions work?

You set a percentage for courses and a fixed amount per fun dive. You can override the percentage for a specific instructor. Marine fees are never part of the commission base.

What happens when the 24-hour demo ends?

The demo uses fictional data stored only in your browser, so there is nothing to clean up. If you want to continue, we set up your own center with your logo, prices and team.

Can I leave and take my data?

Yes. Every list exports to Excel or CSV at any time, and there is no contract.
