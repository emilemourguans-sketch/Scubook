Questions

What dive centres ask us.

Is my customers' data safe?

Each dive centre has its own private database. Only people you invite can sign in, and each person gets a role that limits what they can see. Data is encrypted in transit and stored on managed cloud infrastructure with automatic backups.

Does it work with PADI, or only SSI?

It works with any agency. Certification levels are a list you control, and each customer has a field for their certification number.

Do I need internet at the dive centre?

Yes. WadSea runs in a web browser, so it needs a connection, but it is light enough for a normal mobile data plan.

Can I bring my existing customers and prices?

Yes. Customers and price lists can be imported from CSV or Excel files, and we help you do it during setup.

Can customers pay online?

Not yet. WadSea records the payments you take at the desk, by cash, card or transfer. Online booking and payment are on our roadmap, and founding centres help decide what comes first.

How do instructors' commissions work?

You set a percentage for courses and a fixed amount per fun dive. You can override the percentage for a specific instructor. Marine fees are never part of the commission base.

What happens when the 24-hour demo ends?

The demo uses fictional data stored only in your browser, so there is nothing to clean up. If you want to continue, we set up your own centre with your logo, prices and team.

Can I leave and take my data?

Yes. Every list exports to Excel or CSV at any time, and there is no contract.
