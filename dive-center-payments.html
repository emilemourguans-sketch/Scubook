Questions

Can customers pay online?

Not yet. Scubook records the payments you take at the desk, by cash, card or transfer. Online booking and payment are on our roadmap.

How are marine park fees handled?

Marine park and eco fees are kept apart as cash, so your revenue stays clean. They are tracked separately, with paid confirmation.

How much does Scubook cost?

One plan, everything included: €89 per month per dive center. Pay yearly and save 20%: €71 per month, billed €854 per year. One flat price per dive center, never per user. Prices exclude VAT, which is added at checkout depending on your country.
